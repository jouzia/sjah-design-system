## Asset rules
- Use only approved originals; do not invent logo artwork or hotlink unknown domains.
- Record source, license, dimensions, and light/dark suitability for third-party assets.
- Prefer SVG for simple logos/icons and optimized PNG/WebP for complex raster art.
- Email clients have inconsistent CSS/image support; use a hosted HTTPS image or CID attachment and test with real clients.
